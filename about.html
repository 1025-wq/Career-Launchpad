About Career Launchpad

We believe all students deserve a fair shot at a fulfilling career, regardless of background, connections, or experience.

The Problem We're Solving

South Africa has one of the highest youth unemployment rates in the world. Too many talented students graduate without the tools, guidance, or visibility they need to land their first role.

45.5%
Youth unemployment rate in SA
62%
Graduates who struggle to find work within 12 months
3 in 4
Employers say graduates lack practical skills

Career Launchpad bridges the gap between education and employment by giving students a platform to showcase their skills, get real feedback, and connect with mentors who have walked the same path.
